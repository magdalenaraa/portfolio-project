JapanView Renewal Flyer Design

Flyers, known as chirashi (チラシ) in Japanese, are still widely used in Japan—if you don’t check your mailbox for a week, you’ll probably find it stuffed with them! Drawing from that local reality, I collaborated on end-to-end print design to produce a flyer announcing the rebranding of Japan View, balancing authentic Japanese print culture with our modern brand identity.
